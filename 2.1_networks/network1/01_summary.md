## 📘 1. Summary of the Task
Describe the purpose of this activity in your own words:  
- What is the goal of the lab?  
- What network are you expected to create?

The goal of this lab is to design, configure, and verify a basic Local Area Network (LAN). Throughout this activity, I am expected to build a network topology connecting end devices (such as PCs) through a central network device (such as a switch or router), assign valid static IPv4 addresses and subnet masks to each interface, and test end-to-end connectivity using tools like ping to ensure all devices can communicate properly.
