## 🧠 5. Reflection Questions

### 5.1 What would happen if two PCs had the same IP address?

An IP conflict occurs, causing a loss of network connection for both devices.

### 5.2 Why do we use a switch instead of directly connecting PCs together?

A switch connects multiple devices easily and manages traffic better than a direct connection.

### 5.3 What does the subnet mask 255.255.255.0 indicate?

It shows that the first three numbers are the network ID and the last number is for the individual device
